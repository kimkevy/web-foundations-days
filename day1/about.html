About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight and intuitive web application designed to help you organize your daily thoughts, tasks, and ideas before they slip away.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the input box on the Home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button to save it to your list.
  3. Review and manage your notes as you complete your tasks.

Features

  • Fast and simple note entry
  • Live note counter
  • Clean, accessible user interface
  • Keyboard shortcuts for productivity
